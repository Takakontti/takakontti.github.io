### All Ears achievement tracker
{: data-i18n="trackerTitle"}

An ordered checklist for the **All Ears** achievement. Follow optional NPC conversations in quest order, check off what you’ve heard, and keep track of important quest conditions along the way.
{: data-i18n="trackerIntro"}

Features include per-character progress, search, act-by-act navigation, and an option to hide completed steps. Progress can stay temporary or be saved locally in your browser if you choose. It is a fan-made tool and is not affiliated with Grinding Gear Games.
{: data-i18n="trackerDetails"}
