<h3 data-i18n="trackerTitle">All Ears achievement tracker</h3>

<p data-i18n="trackerIntro">An ordered checklist for the <strong>All Ears</strong> achievement. Follow optional NPC conversations in quest order, check off what you’ve heard, and keep track of important quest conditions along the way.</p>

<p data-i18n="trackerDetails">Features include per-character progress, search, act-by-act navigation, and an option to hide completed steps. Progress can stay temporary or be saved locally in your browser if you choose. It is a fan-made tool and is not affiliated with Grinding Gear Games.</p>
